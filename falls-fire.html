How did the fire-related spectral signal change right after the Falls Fire — and how quickly did the landscape start to look like itself again?

The Falls Fire started on July 10, 2024, roughly 13 miles northwest of Burns, Oregon. An InciWeb update on August 11 reported 150,320 acres at 81% containment; the cause was listed as human-caused and under investigation. This study follows the burn signal across the Malheur National Forest region and into 2026.

WHY BURN SEVERITY MATTERS

Vegetation and roots stabilise soil, slow water movement, and support infiltration. Where fire removes cover and changes the ground surface, rainfall can produce more runoff, erosion, and sediment movement — raising concern for debris-flow and slope-instability hazards long after the flames are out.
