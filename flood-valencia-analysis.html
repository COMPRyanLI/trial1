The strongest story is the spatial expansion and gradual recession of the water signature.

“The value of the time series is not just locating water once; it is showing how the spatial pattern departs from baseline and then returns.”
EXTERNAL CONSISTENCY CHECK

CEMS independently documented extensive flood impacts in Valencia province — more than 53,000 hectares affected as of October 31, 2024. This supports the broad regional severity of the event, but it is not a pixel-by-pixel validation of the source NDWI maps.

WHAT THE IMAGERY SUPPORTS — AND WHAT STAYS A HYPOTHESIS

Strongly supported

  • Water is spectrally dark in NIR relative to many land surfaces.
  • NDWI highlights a larger water / wet-surface footprint post-event than in the pre-event windows.
  • The strongest changes occur in low-lying agriculture, channels, and the La Albufera system.
  • The 2025 composites show a general movement back toward the pre-event pattern.

Needs validation

  • High NDWI can be open water or very wet surfaces; one fixed threshold is not universally reliable.
  • Long date-window composites can blur the short-lived maximum inundation footprint.
  • Phenology, sediment / turbidity, built surfaces, shadow, and cloud can all affect index values.
  • Persistent summer anomalies suggest saturation or drainage issues, but imagery alone can’t attribute the cause.
