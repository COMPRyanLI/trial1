How did the surface-water pattern around Valencia change — before the flood, immediately after it, and through 2025?

The October 29, 2024 event was driven by an intense DANA-related rainfall episode. AEMET reported 771.8 mm in the civil day at Turis, including about 185 mm in a single hour. The Copernicus Emergency Management Service (CEMS) was activated to map flood extent and impacts across the Valencia region.

WHY REMOTE SENSING HERE

The footprint is regional and the response evolves quickly. Repeated satellite observations let the narrative move from a one-day disaster snapshot to a full sequence: a pre-event baseline, event expansion, and recovery.
