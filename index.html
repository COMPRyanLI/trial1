01 / ABOUT

A physicist who ships software

I’m Ryan — a computer science and physics student at Wesleyan University, dual-enrolled through Dartmouth’s Thayer School of Engineering. I like problems where the physics of a measurement and the engineering of a system have to agree with each other.

Most of my work sits in three overlapping places: applied machine learning, platform and agent engineering, and experimental physics. Lately that has meant a backend internship building retrieval systems at PhysaFlow, agent-tooling side projects, plasma-spectroscopy research, and the remote-sensing case studies on this page — reading the land through Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1.

The two studies below are built the way I like to work: state the question, show the signal, then say plainly what the data can and cannot support.
