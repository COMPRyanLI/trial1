CORE OPTICAL INDEX
NBR = (NIR − SWIR) / (NIR + SWIR)
For Sentinel-2, a common implementation uses B8 (NIR, 842 nm) and B12 (SWIR, 2190 nm). Healthy vegetation has strong NIR and lower SWIR; burned or dry surfaces reverse that contrast.
CHANGE PRODUCT
dNBR = NBRpre − NBRpost
dNBR suppresses stable background and emphasises pixels whose NBR changed strongly after fire. Higher positive dNBR generally means larger fire-related change — but severity thresholds should be validated for local vegetation.
TIME-SERIES SCOPE

A pre/post reference, then eight two-month windows: Jan–Feb 2025; Mar–Apr; May–Jun; Jul–Aug; Sep–Oct; Nov–Dec; Jan–Feb 2026; Mar–Apr 2026. A focused Jul 21–Aug 21, 2025 window was also examined.
